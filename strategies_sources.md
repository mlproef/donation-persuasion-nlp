## 1. Exchange / Incentives

**Определение:** Donation request framed as an exchange: reward, gift, return favor, or owed obligation.

**Стратегий в категории:** 5

---

### 1.1. Rewarding Activity

**Описание:** persuader promises or offers a reward, benefit, or special treatment if the other person agrees to donate.

**Подкласс:** Reward / Benefit (Conditional Reward)

**Маркеры:** if you donate, you'll get, access, prize, bonus, discount, raffle

**Источники:**  Marwell & Schmitt, Dimensions of Compliance-Gaining Behavior: An Empirical Analysis (1967) (https://operationpsychological.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/dimensions-of-compliance-gaining-behavior.pdf)

- 

---

### 1.2. Pre-giving

**Описание:** persuader gives or offers something first, or refers to a previous favor, and then asks the other person to donate in return.

**Подкласс:** Pre-giving (Gift First)

**Маркеры:** I already did, I helped you, here you go, here's for you, I did it for you

**Источники:**  Marwell & Schmitt (1967) (https://operationpsychological.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/dimensions-of-compliance-gaining-behavior.pdf)

- 

---

### 1.3. Reciprocity

**Описание:** persuader reminds the other person that they have already done something for them and now expect a donation in return.

**Подкласс:** Reciprocity (Return Favor Reminder)

**Маркеры:** remember I, helped you out, mutually, as a friend

**Источники:** Cialdini, Influence: Science and Practice (1984/2009)  https://ia800203.us.archive.org/33/items/ThePsychologyOfPersuasion/The%20Psychology%20of%20Persuasion.pdf

- 

---

### 1.4. Debt

**Описание:** persuader suggests that the other person owes them something and therefore should donate.

**Подкласс:** Debt (You Owe)

**Маркеры:** you owe, you must, in debt, pay back, time to return

**Источники:** Marwell & Schmitt (1967) (https://operationpsychological.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/dimensions-of-compliance-gaining-behavior.pdf)

- 

---

### 1.5. Promise

**Описание:** persuader explicitly promises some benefit, reward, or positive outcome if the other person donates.

**Подкласс:** Reward / Benefit (Conditional Reward) - Promise variant

**Маркеры:** I promise, I guarantee, you'll get, if you donate

**Источники:**Marwell & Schmitt (1967) (https://operationpsychological.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/dimensions-of-compliance-gaining-behavior.pdf)

-

## 2. Authority / Expertise

**Определение:** Uses perceived credibility: expertise, official status, or transparency proofs.

**Стратегий в категории:** 4

---

### 2.1. Expertise

**Описание:** persuader presents themselves as knowledgeable or experienced and uses their expert status to convince the other person to donate.

**Подкласс:** Expertise (Personal Knowledge)

**Маркеры:** I understand, I worked, from experience, I know how it works

**Источники:**  Marwell & Schmitt (1967) (https://operationpsychological.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/dimensions-of-compliance-gaining-behavior.pdf)

- 

---

### 2.2. Authority

**Описание:** persuader refers to their official position, status, or expert role to pressure the other person into donating.

**Подкласс:** Authority (Official Status)

**Маркеры:** I'm a representative, official collection, administration, regulations, foundation

**Источники:**  French, J. R. P., & Raven, B. (1959). The bases of social power.

---

## 4. Commitment / Consistency

**Определение:** Uses past promises, intentions, or self-image consistency to push donation.

**Стратегий в категории:** 4

---

### 4.1. Activation of Personal Commitment

**Описание:** persuader reminds the other person about their past promises, intentions, or self-image in order to push them to donate now.

**Подкласс:** Past Promise / Prior Intention

**Маркеры:** you promised, you wanted, you said you'd help, we agreed

**Источники:** Marwell & Schmitt (1967) 1 (https://operationpsychological.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/dimensions-of-compliance-gaining-behavior.pdf)
<!-- Добавьте ссылки на источники здесь -->
- 

---

### 4.2. Commitment and Consistency

**Описание:** persuader stresses that the other person should act in line with their previous statements, values, or small agreements by donating now.

**Подкласс:** Self-Image Consistency

**Маркеры:** you're a good person, you're not like that, you always help, this is about you

**Источники:** Cialdini, Influence: Science and Practice  https://www.researchgate.net/publication/229067982_Influence_Science_and_Practice

<!-- Добавьте ссылки на источники здесь -->
- 

---

### 4.3. Foot-in-the-door

**Описание:** persuader first gets the other person to agree to a small request and then escalates to asking for a donation or a larger amount.

**Подкласс:** Past Promise / Prior Intention - Escalation variant

**Маркеры:** you promised, you wanted, first, then, now

**Источники:** Freedman & Fraser, Compliance without Pressure (1966)  https://www.bulidomics.com/w/images/6/6c/Freedman_fraser_footinthedoor_jpsp1966.pdf
<!-- Добавьте ссылки на источники здесь -->
- 

---

### 4.4. Door-in-the-face

**Описание:** persuader starts with a very large or unreasonable request, expects a refusal, and then follows up with a smaller donation request that seems more acceptable.

**Подкласс:** Past Promise / Prior Intention - Door-in-the-face variant

**Маркеры:** first, then, less

**Источники:** Cialdini et al., Reciprocal Concessions (1975) https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Influence_Compliance/Cialdini.et.al.Reciprocal.Concessions.Procedure.1975.article.pdf

## 5. Social Influence

**Определение:** Uses group behavior, belonging, or social comparison.

**Стратегий в категории:** 3

---

### 5.1. Social Proof

**Описание:** persuader emphasizes that many other people donate or support this cause, and therefore the listener should also donate.

**Подкласс:** Social Proof

**Маркеры:** already donated, many supported, everyone chipped in, hundreds of people

**Источники:** Cialdini, Influence: Science and Practice https://www.researchgate.net/publication/229067982_Influence_Science_and_Practice
<!-- Добавьте ссылки на источники здесь -->
- 

---

### 5.2. Unity

**Описание:** persuader emphasizes a shared identity or group membership, presenting donating as something that 'people like us' do.

**Подкласс:** Unity (Shared Identity)

**Маркеры:** we together, ours, people like us, our own, our community

**Источники:** Tajfel, H., & Turner, J. C. (1979). An integrative theory of intergroup conflict.
Turner, J. C., et al. (1987). Rediscovering the Social Group
<!-- Добавьте ссылки на источники здесь -->
- 

---

### 5.3. Social Positioning

**Описание:** persuader implies that certain kinds of people donate and others do not, placing the listener in or out of a desired social group.

**Подкласс:** Social Positioning (Labeling)

**Маркеры:** normal people, decent, who really, don't be the one who

**Источники:**  Festinger, Leon (1954) “A Theory of Social Comparison Processes” Human Relations https://www2.psych.ubc.ca/~schaller/528Readings/Festinger1954.pdf

## 6. Rational / Impact Appeal

**Стратегий в категории:** 2

---

### 6.1. Rational Appeal

**Подкласс:** Facts & Numbers

**Маркеры:** %, €, $, rub, according to data, statistics, numbers

**Источники:** Chaiken, S. (1980). Heuristic versus systematic information processing. JPSP.
Bekkers, R., & Wiepking, P. (2011). A literature review of empirical studies of philanthropy. Nonprofit and Voluntary Sector Quarterly.

- 

---

### 6.2. Logical Appeal

**Подкласс:** Efficiency / Impact

**Маркеры:** every 10€, maximum effect, most effective, impact, matching

## 7. Emotional Influence

**Определение:** Uses emotions: empathy, pity, stories, emotional pressure.

**Стратегий в категории:** 6

---

### 7.1. Emotional Appeal

**Описание:** persuader tries to move the other person emotionally, for example by appealing to compassion, sadness, hope, or pride to get a donation.

**Подкласс:** Emotional Appeal (General)

**Маркеры:** pity, suffering, heart, imagine, crying, story, pain

**Источники:** Slovic, P. (2007). If I look at the mass I will never act.
Damasio, A. (1994). Descartes’ Error.

- 

---

### 7.2. Storytelling

**Описание:** persuader tells a story or narrative, often about a person or situation, to emotionally illustrate why a donation is needed.

**Подкласс:** Storytelling

**Маркеры:** story, yesterday, with him, she, family, child, happened

**Источники:** Green & Brock, The Role of Transportation in the Persuasiveness of Public Narratives (2000) https://www.researchgate.net/publication/12248972_The_Role_of_Transportation_in_the_Persuasiveness_of_Public_Narratives проверить еще раз 

- 

---

### 7.3. Empathy Appeal

**Описание:** persuader asks the other person to imagine the suffering or feelings of others and to donate out of compassion.

**Подкласс:** Empathy (Perspective Taking)

**Маркеры:** imagine, put yourself, how would you, if it were your

**Источники:** Batson et al., Empathy and Altruism (1991)  https://www.researchgate.net/publication/313391047_The_empathy-altruism_hypothesis

- 

---

### 7.4. Sympathy Appeal

**Описание:** persuader highlights their own suffering or the hardship of others to make the listener feel sorry and want to donate.

**Подкласс:** Sympathy / Pity

**Маркеры:** poor thing, unfortunate, pity, no strength, terrible to see

**Источники:** Batson, C. D., et al. (1987). Distress and empathy. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 8. Urgency / Scarcity

**Определение:** Pressures donation by limited time or limited opportunity/slots/resources.

**Стратегий в категории:** 3

---

### 8.1. Urgency

**Описание:** persuader stresses that time is running out and that the donation must happen immediately.

**Подкласс:** Urgency (Time Pressure)

**Маркеры:** urgent, right now, today, hours left, until midnight, immediately

**Источники:**  Cialdini, Influence: Science and Practice https://www.researchgate.net/publication/229067982_Influence_Science_and_Practice вопрос точно ли 
<!-- Добавьте ссылки на источники здесь -->
- 

---

### 8.2. Scarcity

**Описание:** persuader stresses that time, resources, or opportunities are limited, so the person should donate now before it is too late.

**Подкласс:** Scarcity (Limited Quantity/Opportunity)

**Маркеры:** limited, limit, spots, only N, matching, quota

**Источники:**  Cialdini, Influence: Science and Practice https://www.researchgate.net/publication/229067982_Influence_Science_and_Practice
<!-- Добавьте ссылки на источники здесь -->

**Подкласс:** Scarcity (Limited Quantity/Opportunity) - Manipulation variant

## 9. Threat / Pressure

**Определение:** Uses explicit or implicit negative consequences or persistent discomfort to force donation.

**Стратегий в категории:** 5

---

### 9.1. Threat

**Описание:** persuader explicitly warns that negative consequences will follow if the other person does not donate.

**Подкласс:** Threat (Sanction by Persuader)

**Маркеры:** otherwise I'll block, I'll complain, there will be consequences, last warning

**Источники:** Threat → Marwell & Schmitt (1967) (https://operationpsychological.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/dimensions-of-compliance-gaining-behavior.pdf)
<!-- Добавьте ссылки на источники здесь -->
- 

---

### 9.2. Aversive Stimulation

**Описание:** persuader creates or maintains discomfort, tension, or unpleasant pressure until the other person agrees to donate.

**Подкласс:** Aversive Pressure (Persistent Discomfort)

**Маркеры:** what's up, when will you transfer, I'm waiting, don't ignore, answer now

**Источники:** Festinger, L. (1957). A Theory of Cognitive Dissonance.
Brehm, J. W. (1966). A Theory of Psychological Reactance.
Dolinski, D. (2016). Techniques of social influence.
<!-- Добавьте ссылки на источники здесь -->
- 

---

### 9.3. Punishing Activity

**Описание:** persuader warns that something bad or unpleasant will happen if the other person refuses to donate.

**Подкласс:** Threat (Sanction by Persuader) - Punishment variant

**Маркеры:** otherwise, bad, unpleasant, otherwise I'll block

**Источники:** Marwell & Schmitt (1967)  (https://operationpsychological.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/dimensions-of-compliance-gaining-behavior.pdf)
<!-- Добавьте ссылки на источники здесь -->
- 

---

### 9.4. Overloading

**Описание:** persuader presents a large amount of information, details, or options in a way that overwhelms the other person and nudges them to donate.

**Подкласс:** Aversive Pressure (Persistent Discomfort) - Overloading variant

**Маркеры:** many, all, details, options

**Источники:** Iyengar, S. S., & Lepper, M. R. (2000). When choice is demotivating. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
<!-- Добавьте ссылки на источники здесь -->
- 

---

### 9.5. Confusion Induction

**Описание:** persuader makes things complicated, unclear, or contradictory so that the other person is disoriented and more likely to comply and donate.

**Подкласс:** Aversive Pressure (Persistent Discomfort) - Confusion variant

**Маркеры:** unclear, complicated, contradiction

**Источники:** найти Fennis, Das, Pruyn (2004) “If you can’t convince them, confuse them” Journal of Consumer Psychology

## 11. Framing & Presentation

**Определение:** Presents situation in particular way to make donating attractive.

**Стратегий в категории:** 4

---

### 11.1. Framing

**Описание:** persuader presents the same situation or outcome in a particular way to make donating look more attractive or reasonable.

**Подкласс:** Framing

**Маркеры:** how, instead, better, simpler

**Источники:** Tversky & Kahneman, The Framing of Decisions (1981) https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/gelman/surveys.course/TverskyKahneman1981.pdf
<!-- Добавьте ссылки на источники здесь -->
- 

---

### 11.2. Loss Aversion Appeal

**Описание:** persuader emphasizes what will be lost or missed if the other person does not donate.

**Подкласс:** Loss Aversion Appeal

**Маркеры:** you'll lose, you'll miss, won't get

**Источники:** Kahneman, Daniel; Tversky, Amos (1979) “Prospect Theory: An Analysis of Decision under Risk” https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_Tversky_1979_Prospect_theory.pdf
